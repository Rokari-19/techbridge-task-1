A PRACTICAL 30-DAY EXPERIENCE

Not just lessons.
Things you can build.

TechBridge's 30-day internship is designed around practical work. You'll progressively develop your web development skills by completing a sequence of projects, with each task adding another piece to the experience.

Start with the structure and styling of a website, then move into interactive features, registration, submissions, dashboards, and finally a connected platform.

↗Follow the sequence. Each milestone introduces a new part of the build.

Your progress,
step by step.
